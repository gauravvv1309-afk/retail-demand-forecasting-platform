# Problem Understanding

## 1. Problem in My Own Words

This project is about building a retail demand forecasting platform for a retail company that operates multiple stores and sells many products.

The main problem is that the company does not always know how much stock will be needed in the future. Because of this, some products may be overstocked, which means too much inventory is purchased or stored. Other products may go out of stock, which means the company loses potential sales and customers may not find what they want.

The purpose of the system is to predict future product demand so that the company can make better decisions about inventory, purchasing, and stock planning.

## 2. Who I Believe the Users Will Be

I believe the users of this system may include:

- Inventory managers
- Supply chain or procurement teams
- Store managers
- Business analysts
- Operations managers
- Senior management

These users would use the demand forecasts to support planning and decision-making.

## 3. What Decisions the Forecast Will Support

The forecast is expected to support decisions such as:

- How much stock should be ordered
- Which products should be replenished
- Which stores may require more inventory
- Which items may face stockout risk
- Which items may be overstocked
- How inventory can be distributed across stores

## 4. What the System Should Produce as Output

I believe the system should produce outputs such as:

- Forecasted demand for each product
- Forecasted demand by store
- Forecasted demand for a future time period such as daily, weekly, or monthly
- Reports or dashboards showing expected demand trends
- Alerts or indicators for possible overstocking or stockouts

## 5. What Is Currently Unknown

At this stage, many important details are not yet known. These include:

- What dataset will be provided
- What data sources are available
- What the exact business rules are
- How many stores and products are involved
- What time granularity is needed (daily, weekly, monthly)
- How far into the future the forecast should predict
- Who will use the system most often
- Whether the system needs a dashboard, API, or internal application
- What the deployment requirements are
- What accuracy level is expected
- What constraints or deadlines exist for prediction

## 6. Assumptions I Am Currently Making

At this stage, I am making the following assumptions:

- Historical sales data will be available
- Product and store information will be available
- Date or time information will be available
- The company wants better stock planning
- Forecasts will help reduce overstocking and stockouts
- The first version of the system will likely be simple and practical rather than highly advanced
- The forecast may eventually be used inside a dashboard or business application
- Data quality issues may exist and will need to be checked before model development
